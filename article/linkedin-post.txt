Can you run a 27B-parameter LLM on an 8 GB laptop GPU?

Yes, with AirLLM. I benchmarked how well it works.

7 open models (Gemma 4 and Qwen 3.x, 2B to 27B parameters) on:
• my RTX 2070 laptop (Linux)
• my GTX 1070 laptop (Windows 11)
• a free Tesla T4 notebook on Kaggle

What I found:

→ Every model runs. The 27B ones need only 3.7 GB of VRAM, because AirLLM streams the model one layer at a time.

→ Out of the box it does 0.01–0.16 tokens/s, and its 4-bit mode turns Gemma 4 into gibberish.

→ Two small fixes make it 2–38× faster: NF4 on the weight matrices only, and no gc.collect() after every layer.

→ Free RAM matters more than the GPU. The 27B models ran 2.5× faster on Kaggle's T4 than on my faster laptop, because there their 15 GB of layer files fit in the page cache.

→ Even a Pascal-era GTX 1070 runs all seven models.

→ For comparison, vLLM on an A100 or H100 (Modal) runs the same models 57–545× faster, because the whole model sits in GPU memory.

→ Plan for minutes per answer: 2–4 minutes for 200 tokens from a model up to 12B, 16–41 minutes from a 27B.

AirLLM lets a laptop run models that do not fit its GPU. It is for batch jobs and experiments, not for chat.
